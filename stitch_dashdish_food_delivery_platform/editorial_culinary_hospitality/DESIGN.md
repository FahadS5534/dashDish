## Brand & Style
The design system positions food ordering not as a chaotic transactional gig economy utility, but as an intimate, curated dining experience translated to the screen. Drawing aesthetic codes from independent culinary journals, heritage menus, and high-touch boutique hospitality, it treats dishes, culinary artisans, and ingredients as subjects of editorial reverence.

### Emotional Landscape
- **Epicurean & Intentional:** Evokes the sensory warmth of fresh bread, reduction sauces, and table linens rather than cold algorithmic speed.
- **Authoritative & Curated:** Inspires confidence through decisive typographic hierarchy, generous breathing room, and structured compositions.
- **Tactile Comfort:** Soft, organic surfaces and warm ivory backdrops eliminate digital fatigue, grounding the user in an inviting, analog-inspired space.

### Design Movement: Modern Editorial Tactile
The system balances two visual philosophies:
1. **Culinary Editorial:** Expressive high-contrast serifs, deliberate asymmetric hero layouts, and rich charcoal micro-typography reminiscent of high-end food periodicals.
2. **Warm Functional Minimalism:** Crisp geometric interface controls, 1px warm borders, flat low-elevation containers, and zero novelty artifacts (no synthetic glows, no gradients, and no tech-centric skeuomorphism).

## Colors
The color palette avoids cold digital neutrals and strictly excludes cool blues, cyans, and tech-purples. Every tone is derived from organic gastronomy: vine-ripened tomatoes, unbleached linen, cast iron, kitchen herbs, and roasted grains.

### Roles & Application
- **Primary Accent (`#D9381E`, Active `#C83319`):** Reserved for singular primary actions (checkout, add to order), dynamic price presentation, and focused validation states. It operates as a distinct focal point against warm backdrops.
- **Canvas & Surface Tier:**
  - Base Canvas: Soft Warm Linen (`#FAF8F5`) — replaces stark clinical `#FFFFFF`.
  - Elevated Container: Warm Ivory (`#F5F1EA`) — applied to card surfaces, drawers, and modal backdrops.
  - Recessed / Inset Surface: Pale Biscuit (`#EFECE6`) — used for input backgrounds, segmented pickers, and tag containers.
- **Neutral Dark / Text Hierarchy:**
  - Primary Body & Headings: Charcoal Umber (`#1F1C19`) — near-black with brown-red warmth ensuring rich contrast without sterile digital harshness.
  - Secondary Text / Meta: Muted Walnut (`#544E48`) — used for preparation times, descriptions, and dietary annotations.
  - Dividers & Outlines: Pale Sandstone (`#E8E3DC`) — crisp 1px structural framing.
- **Culinary Semantic Accents:**
  - Earthy Sage Green (`#5E7A68`, Pale Tint `#EAF0EB`): Order delivered, kitchen open, verified ingredients, organic indicators.
  - Soft Amber Gold (`#D98A2C`, Pale Tint `#FAF1E4`): In-prep status, pastry badges, chef's specials, active ratings.
  - Warm Terracotta (`#C96A52`): Secondary alerts, low-stock warnings, and informational culinary footnotes.

## Typography
Typography is the cornerstone of this design system's editorial sensibility. The tension between the graceful high-contrast editorial serif (`Playfair Display`) and the rational, monoline clarity of `DM Sans` creates an intentional print-journal experience.

### Rules of Usage
- **Serif (`Playfair Display`):** Exclusively utilized for restaurant names, dish titles, narrative curated collections (e.g., "The Morning Patisserie", "Handcrafted Pastas"), and editorial intro moments. Never use serif for pricing, utility controls, forms, or metadata.
- **Sans (`DM Sans`):** Powers all interactive components, nutritional counts, cart sums, preparation tracking, system labels, and navigational anchors.
- **Price Display:** Rendered in `DM Sans` bold with tabular figures enabled (`font-variant-numeric: tabular-nums`) to ensure vertical alignment throughout ordering receipts and item listings.
- **Case Conventions:** Eyebrow tags and section categories leverage `label-sm` with full uppercase tracking (`+0.05em`), evoking the cataloging style of fine menus.

## Layout & Spacing
A disciplined 8pt spatial grid dictates all layout calculations. The layout architecture treats negative space as a luxury material, preventing the cluttered feeling common in rapid food-ordering platforms.

### Breakpoints & Layout Models
- **Mobile (`0 - 767px`):** Fluid single-column layout. Margin set to `1.25rem` (20px), gutter to `1rem` (16px). Bottom sheets replace centered modals. Bottom-anchored persistent order bars maintain immediate access without obscuring dish discovery.
- **Tablet (`768px - 1023px`):** 8-column grid with `1.5rem` gutters. Accommodates two-column restaurant listings and split checkout layouts.
- **Desktop (`1024px+`):** 12-column fixed-max grid clamped at `1280px` centered with minimum canvas margins of `3rem`. Dish grids default to 3 or 4 columns; restaurant hero overviews use an asymmetric 7/5 split between imagery and menu navigation.

### Rhythm & Composition
- Maintain generous vertical offsets (`space-xl` / 40px) between menu courses (Starters, Mains, Dessert, Beverage pairings) to preserve clear narrative chapters.
- Component-level spacing uses `space-xs` (4px) and `space-sm` (8px) for tightly coupled meta tags (cook time, dietary badges), while cards use `space-md` (16px) or `space-lg` (24px) internal padding.

## Elevation & Depth
Depth in this design system is structural and tactile rather than atmospheric or simulated. The system deliberately rejects heavy directional dropshadows, colored glows, and blurred neon reflections.

### Surface Tiers & Contours
- **Surface Contours:** Containers gain boundary definition through explicit, subtle 1px strokes tinted in Pale Sandstone (`#E8E3DC`). 
- **Ambient Shadow Character:** Where physical detachment is required (such as sticky cart drawers, floating floating food modifier sheets, and interactive card hovers), shadows use a diffused, warm-tinted falloff:
  - Default Card Ambient: `0 2px 8px -1px rgba(45, 41, 38, 0.04)`
  - Floating Control / Hover Surface: `0 4px 20px -2px rgba(45, 41, 38, 0.06)`
  - Elevated Sheet / Modal: `0 12px 32px -4px rgba(45, 41, 38, 0.10)`
- **Tonal Layering:** Visual depth is primarily established by alternating canvas hues. Background is `#FAF8F5`, resting cards are `#F5F1EA`, and nested controls within cards sit on `#EFECE6` or flat white `#FFFFFF`.

## Shapes
The shape language is tailored, architectural, and restrained. It deliberately avoids fully circular, hyper-bubble pill treatments across the entire UI to prevent a cartoonish or hyper-casual appearance.

### Geometry Specifications
- **Cards & Culinary Modules:** `12px` border radius (`rounded-lg`). Provides an approachable edge while retaining formal grid discipline.
- **Form Controls & Inputs:** `8px` border radius. Ensures input fields feel stable and unambiguous.
- **Primary & Secondary Buttons:** `8px` border radius. Balanced, tactile, and distinct from novelty round chips.
- **Micro Tags & Dietary Badges:** `4px` or `6px` slight radius for clean tabular alignment.
- **Floating Counter Incrementors:** `8px` bounded containers with internal hairline dividers.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary:** Solid Tomato-Red (`#D9381E`) background, crisp white text (`#FFFFFF`), `8px` corner radius, `0` border, `12px 20px` padding for standard actions. Hover state transitions to Deep Burnt-Coral (`#C83319`).
- **Secondary:** Warm Linen (`#F5F1EA`) surface with a 1px Sandstone border (`#E8E3DC`), charcoal text (`#1F1C19`). Hover state darkens background to `#EFECE6`.
- **Text / Editorial Link:** Muted Charcoal text with an underline offset of 4px in Tomato-Red (`#D9381E`). Never displays an icon arrow unless linking out of section.

### Dish & Restaurant Cards
- **Construction:** Flat warm surface (`#F5F1EA` or `#FFFFFF` on linen backdrop), wrapped in a 1px `#E8E3DC` hairline border with `12px` corner radius.
- **Image Integration:** 4:3 or 16:9 aspect ratio dish photography with a slight warm color grade. Direct image corners match parent top corners (`12px 12px 0 0`) with zero gap or padding.
- **Content Hierarchy:** Dish name in `Playfair Display` (`headline-sm`), description in `DM Sans` (`body-md`) with 2-line clamp, followed by tabular price and an unobtrusive `+` quick-add button placed at the lower right.

### Chips & Filtering Tags
- **Default State:** `#EFECE6` background, 1px transparent border, `#544E48` text, `6px` radius.
- **Active / Selected State:** Solid Deep Charcoal (`#1F1C19`) background with Ivory (`#FAF8F5`) text.
- **Dietary Indicators:** Subtle organic color tags (e.g., Earthy Sage `#EAF0EB` background with `#5E7A68` text for Vegan/Gluten-Free).

### Input Fields & Controls
- **Field Anatomy:** Single-line height of `48px`, `8px` radius, background in `#FAF8F5`, framed by 1px `#E8E3DC` border. Label in `label-md` placed above input.
- **Focus State:** 1px border shift to Charcoal (`#1F1C19`) with no high-glow ring or colored shadow halos.
- **Checkboxes & Radios:** Unchecked state is a 1.5px `#E8E3DC` outline on `#FAF8F5`. Checked state is a solid Charcoal (`#1F1C19`) fill with a crisp `#FAF8F5` interior checkmark or concentric dot.

### Specialized Culinary Components
- **Quantity Stepper:** Compact grouped unit with an `8px` container radius, `#EFECE6` background, composed of minus button, centered tabular digit (`title-md`), and plus button, separated by faint 1px hairline dividers.
- **Order Tracker (Hospitality Status):** Linear progress line using warm earthy states (Received: Amber `#D98A2C`, In Kitchen: Terracotta `#C96A52`, In Transit / Plated: Sage `#5E7A68`), avoiding neon tech maps.
- **Curated Tasting Menu Strip:** Horizontal editorial ribbon pairing oversized Playfair numerals (`01`, `02`, `03`) with curated multi-course narrative selections.
